Government departments and public sector enterprises on the [Government e-Marketplace (GeM)](https://gem.gov.in/) do not buy consumer media players because built-in operating system players and VLC already fulfill general desktop playback for free. To sell your media player on GeM, you must pivot from a consumer player to a specialized enterprise software that solves specific institutional compliance, surveillance, presentation, or display use cases. [1, 2, 3] 
Government tenders and direct purchases for video/media playback typically fall into four distinct categories:
Many government offices, transit hubs, and public institutions regularly procure player software to power digital notice boards, information kiosks, and smart displays. [2, 4] 

* 
* Scheduled CMS Playback: Support running automated video and image playlists on a schedule (e.g., looping official messages from 9 AM to 6 PM). [2, 5] 
* Kiosk / Lockdown Mode: Run in true full-screen with system keys locked (disabling Alt+Tab, Escape, or OS navigation so the public cannot exit the player).
* Multi-Format Splits: Enable multi-zone split-screen displays (e.g., showing a live RTMP/RTSP stream on one side, an automated PDF/PPT ticker on the bottom, and pre-recorded video on the other). [2] 
* Auto-Start & Remote Health: Automatic boot-up playback upon power cycle and crash-recovery reporting over a local LAN. [5] 
* 

Police departments, defense units, municipal corporations, and courtrooms frequently require specialized playback software for surveillance audits and evidence presentation. [1, 6, 7] 

* 
* Proprietary & Multi-Camera Synced Playback: Synchronized multi-angle playback that can open and play CCTV feeds (H.264/H.265/DAV/raw streams) with synchronized timestamp timelines.
* Tamper Verification & Digital Watermarking: Built-in cryptographic hash verification (SHA-256) to prove the video file was not edited or altered, along with dynamic on-screen watermarking (user name, IP address, and system date/time).
* Frame-by-Frame Forensic Controls: Millisecond precision frame stepping, digital zoom on specific coordinate sectors without quality distortion, and instant clipping/redaction export. [1, 3] 
* 

Government colleges, training academies, and administrative conference rooms buy presentation solutions designed for interactive touchpanels and podium displays. [8] 

* 
* Interactive Annotation Over Video: Real-time pen, highlight, and drawing tools directly over playing or paused video.
* Multi-Screen Casting & Dual Output: Separate "Presenter View" (showing controls, notes, and upcoming media) and "Audience View" (clean video feed sent via HDMI or wireless casting).
* Offline-First Secure Delivery: Complete offline operation with no background internet pings or telemetry, which is mandatory for air-gapped defense and administrative setups. [4] 
* 

PSUs and ministries procure media engines for training and internal communication that must prevent leakage of confidential material. [1] 

* 
* Encrypted File Playback: Media files stored as encrypted blobs (AES-256) that can only be decrypted and rendered inside your proprietary player.
* Screen Capture & Recording Prevention: Blocking screen-capture tools (OBS, Snipping Tool, Zoom share) from recording the video playback window.
* Active Directory / Single Sign-On (SSO): Integration with corporate directory credentials (LDAP/AD) to authenticate who is watching which file. [1] 
* 

Beyond software features, winning bids or listing on the GeM Portal requires specific vendor compliance:

* 
* Make in India (MII) Compliance: You must self-certify or provide an auditor certificate confirming Class-I or Class-II local content (IP ownership and code developed in India).
* Vulnerability Assessment Certification: Software purchased by government entities typically requires a Safe-to-Host or VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing) clearance from a [CERT-In empanelled auditor](https://www.cert-in.org.in/).
* Offline Deployment & Packaging: Provide silent offline installers (.msi for Windows, .deb/.rpm for Linux distributions like BOSS Linux or Ubuntu). [5]
